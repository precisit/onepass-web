onepass-web

A runtime and demos for one-pass specialists: models that answer one question per forward pass, with no tokenizer, no generation and no server.

Browser bench

Measures what a specialist costs in a browser: cold start and per-decision latency, for any one-pass ONNX model, wasm against webgpu. This is the instrument behind the numbers below.

Play Connect Four against a specialist
