onepass-web

A runtime and demos for one-pass specialists: models that answer one question per forward pass, with no tokenizer, no generation and no server.

Browser bench

Measures what a specialist costs in a browser: cold start and per-decision latency, for any one-pass ONNX model, wasm against webgpu. This is the instrument behind the numbers below.

Play Connect Four against a specialist

A 706 k-parameter model that scores the legal columns in one forward pass — 3.9 ms per move on webgpu. It plays, it loses, and the column scores under the board show exactly why. The same page runs an arena, so a model can play another model or a naive random opponent, as fast as the browser allows.

706 k parameters, 2.9 MB ONNX, headless Chromium on an M4, model fetched from Hugging Face. The shape is a bigger lever than the provider: shrinking the option block from 40 × 96 to 8 × 24 cut the work about twenty-fold and made the new wasm path faster than the old webgpu one. The runtime is still 29 MB, ten times the model, which is why a purpose-built kernel is on the roadmap. WebNN (the browser's own NPU route) needs Chrome Canary and a flag; it is measured, not assumed.
