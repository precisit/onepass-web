Connect Four: one model, two runtimes

The same 7.4 M parameter Connect Four model as in the main demo, run two ways in your browser: with onnxruntime-web on WebAssembly (what the main demo uses), and with onepass-webgpu, a 22 KB WebGPU runtime written for this kind of model. Play a game: the WebGPU runtime picks the moves, and every move is also timed on WebAssembly.

Time per move

Every AI move is scored by all three engines, one after the other, on the same position. Time is from the board going in to the scores coming back in JavaScript.

Race: 200 positions

Each engine scores the same 200 positions from random games, one at a time, after 10 warm-up moves.

What gets loaded

engineruntime codemodel filestart-up

Start-up = creating the engine from the downloaded file, plus its first move. The model download itself depends on your network and is not included. The WebGPU runtime reads its weights straight from the unchanged fp32 ONNX file.

Technical checks

Before the WebGPU runtime is trusted, it must agree with onnxruntime-web (wasm, same fp32 file) on reference positions. The encoder is also checked against the Python tooling's bytes.
