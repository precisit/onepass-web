Connect Four against a tiny AI in your browser

The AI is a 7.8 MB model that picks each move in a single forward pass: it looks at the board, gives every free column a score and plays the best one. No search, no hand-written rules — and it runs entirely on your device, nothing is sent to a server. Model · How it was trained · Source
